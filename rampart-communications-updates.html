Platform Updates · 2025–2026

What's new at Tradespace — relevant for Rampart Communications

A few updates since we last spoke — each maps directly to the challenge of running patent operations at a defense communications company where budget visibility and cost predictability are non-negotiable.

5 updates worth knowing

Tradespace Counsel

Flat-fee prosecution through a curated network of vetted patent attorneys. Each filing has a fixed, known cost upfront — no billable-hour surprises, no retroactive invoices. For patent writers and IP leads who need to make the case internally, a predictable line-item is a significantly easier conversation than variable outside counsel billing.

AI Filing Event Processing

Upload office actions, notices, or correspondence PDFs and the platform auto-classifies the event, extracts key deadlines, and drafts a response summary. Prosecution administration that used to take hours of analyst time is handled in minutes — without requiring any change to how your team already works.

Finance Module

Every disclosure, filing, and agreement has its own spend and revenue summary with full drill-down into individual records. See exactly where your IP budget is going, by filing and by matter, so budget requests and spend reports are based on actual data rather than estimates.

Portfolio Analytics

Real-time visibility into prosecution status, filing velocity, and portfolio health across every technology area. Track what's active, what's pending, and where the gaps are — without hunting across docketing systems or pulling manual reports.

Departments & Business Units

Segment the portfolio by business unit, program, or product line and track prosecution and spend by each. Useful when defense and commercial programs share an IP operation but need separate reporting and accountability.
