Platform Updates · 2025–2026

What's new at Tradespace — relevant for Chubb

A few platform updates relevant to IP and legal teams at global insurance and financial services companies — each maps to the challenge of maintaining portfolio visibility and prosecution cost control across a diversified, enterprise-scale IP operation.

5 updates worth knowing

Finance Module

Complete spend visibility on every disclosure, filing, and licensing agreement — with drill-downs into individual matters. GCs and IP leaders at financial services companies tell us the board-level conversation around IP investment has become more demanding; Tradespace gives you the data to support that conversation with precision rather than approximation.

Portfolio Analytics

Real-time portfolio health across all technology and IP areas — prosecution status, filing velocity, coverage gaps, and performance by matter type. For a company like Chubb with IP spanning insurtech, data analytics, risk modeling, and enterprise software, a single dashboard replaces scattered reporting across docketing and billing systems.

Tradespace Counsel

Flat-fee prosecution through a vetted outside counsel network. Predictable per-filing costs are especially useful when managing a broad patent portfolio where the cost-benefit of continuing prosecution on individual assets needs to be evaluated regularly — without the billing variability that makes those decisions harder to make on time.

AI Filing Event Processing

Prosecution correspondence is uploaded and automatically classified, with deadlines extracted and response summaries drafted. Reduces the administrative overhead of prosecution management so the in-house IP team can focus on strategy and portfolio decisions rather than tracking filing events.

ROI Calculator

Model the cost-benefit of Tradespace against your current IP management spend — broken down by prosecution cost, attorney time, and administrative overhead. Gives GCs and CLOs the numbers to evaluate the investment before committing to a full deployment.
