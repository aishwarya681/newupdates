Platform Updates · 2025–2026

What's new at Tradespace — relevant for Redpanda

A few platform updates relevant to IP teams at infrastructure and developer tooling companies building their patent program in parallel with a fast-moving product roadmap — each maps to getting foundational IP coverage in place without diverting engineering or leadership attention.

5 updates worth knowing

Disclosure Intake via Slack, Gmail & Notion

Engineers submit disclosures from the tools they already use — Slack, Gmail, or Notion. The platform captures, structures, and routes each one automatically. For a company like Redpanda where the engineering team is moving fast, this removes the friction that causes valuable inventions to go undisclosed simply because the submission process is too cumbersome.

AI Filing Event Processing

Office actions and prosecution correspondence are uploaded and automatically classified, with deadlines extracted and response summaries drafted. Even with a small in-house IP function, prosecution doesn't fall behind — the platform handles the administrative overhead so the team focuses on strategy.

Tradespace Counsel

Flat-fee prosecution through a vetted attorney network with infrastructure software and distributed systems expertise. Predictable per-filing costs give early-stage and growth-phase companies a sustainable way to build a patent portfolio without committing to open-ended outside counsel billing.

Portfolio Analytics

Real-time visibility into prosecution status, filing velocity, and portfolio health across all technology areas. Track where Redpanda's IP coverage is building and where it's lagging behind the product roadmap — so the IP investment stays connected to product and competitive strategy.

Finance Module

Prosecution spend visibility on every filing, broken down by outside counsel and technology area. Gives IP leads and GCs a clear picture of cost per patent as the portfolio grows — useful for board reporting and for making the case for continued IP investment as the company scales.
