Platform Updates · 2025–2026

What's new at Tradespace — relevant for A*STAR

A few platform updates since we were last in conversation — each maps to the operational realities of managing IP across a large, multi-institute research organization where portfolio scale, prosecution cost, and commercialization pipeline all need to be visible in one place.

5 updates worth knowing

Tradespace Counsel

Flat-fee prosecution through a curated network of vetted patent attorneys. For a research institute portfolio that spans biomedical, advanced manufacturing, digital technologies, and more — each in varying stages of prosecution — fixed per-filing costs replace unpredictable billing and make it possible to forecast prosecution spend accurately across the full portfolio.

AI Filing Event Processing

Office actions, patent notices, and correspondence are uploaded and the platform auto-classifies the event, extracts all deadlines, and drafts a response summary. Across a portfolio of A*STAR's scale, this removes the manual tracking burden from IP analysts and ensures nothing slips through when volumes are high.

Finance Module

Spend and revenue summaries on every disclosure, filing, and commercialization agreement — with drill-downs into individual records. Management teams get a clear, real-time view of where the IP budget is going, by institute, by technology domain, and by matter type.

Departments & Business Units

Segment the portfolio by research institute or division and track prosecution status, pipeline health, and spend by each unit. Useful for reporting to institute directors and for identifying where IP creation is ahead of prosecution capacity.

Portfolio Analytics

Real-time visibility into filing velocity, prosecution status, and portfolio health across every technology area. Identify which research domains are generating the most commercially viable IP and where the pipeline needs attention before assets lapse or deadlines are missed.
