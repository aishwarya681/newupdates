Platform Updates · 2025–2026

What's new at Tradespace — relevant for Commvault

A few platform updates relevant to IP teams at enterprise data management companies — each maps to the pressure of keeping prosecution aligned with a software and cloud infrastructure portfolio that's evolving faster than traditional IP workflows can handle.

5 updates worth knowing

AI Filing Event Processing

Upload office actions, USPTO notices, or any prosecution correspondence and the platform auto-classifies the event, extracts all key dates, and drafts a response summary. For a data and cloud IP portfolio with high filing volume across software and infrastructure domains, prosecution administration no longer requires significant analyst time.

Finance Module

Spend and revenue visibility on every disclosure, filing, and agreement — with drill-downs by matter type, outside counsel, and technology area. IP leaders get a real-time view of where the prosecution budget is going, so cost-per-asset conversations with leadership are grounded in actual data, not estimates.

Portfolio Analytics

Real-time portfolio health, filing velocity, and prosecution status across technology areas. Identify where Commvault's portfolio is concentrated, where prosecution is behind, and where assets may be underperforming relative to R&D investment — all in one dashboard without pulling manual reports.

Tradespace Counsel

Flat-fee prosecution through a vetted attorney network with expertise in software, cloud infrastructure, and data protection IP. Predictable per-filing costs replace variable outside counsel billing — making it easier to manage prosecution spend as the portfolio scales with the product roadmap.

Departments & Business Units

Segment the portfolio by product line, engineering team, or business unit and track prosecution and spend by each. Useful when IP strategy needs to align with separate P&L owners across backup, recovery, cloud data management, and security product areas.
