What's new at Tradespace —
built for innovation offices doing more with less

A summary of the capabilities that have evolved this year, focused on what matters most for an innovation office balancing a growing research portfolio, active entrepreneurship programming, and a lean team expected to move disclosures toward commercialization without adding headcount.

New & Updated Capabilities
🤝
Suggested Partners Agent
Rebuilt AI partner matching that identifies companies, investors, and VC firms most likely to license, co-develop, or commercialize a given technology. For each opportunity, the agent proposes up to three partner archetypes specific to the technology's stage and market fit, sources candidates, and scores them by partnership likelihood, technical fit, and financial readiness. For a lean innovation office, this replaces the manual research that typically consumes the most time before a licensing conversation can even start.
Partner Matching
📥
Invention Disclosure Portal
A streamlined faculty-facing intake experience that guides researchers through exactly what the innovation office needs from a disclosure, reducing incomplete submissions and back-and-forth follow-up. For an office where the innovation mandate sits alongside entrepreneurship programming, a cleaner intake process means the team spends its time evaluating what came in rather than chasing down missing information from busy faculty.
Faculty Intake
⚖️
Managed Filing Service
AI-assisted patent drafting combined with attorney review and filing through a vetted network of 250+ patent attorneys. Provisionals from $1,000 and utility applications under $5,000, drafted and filed in under a week. For a growing research program where every filing dollar has to be justified, this removes the cost and capacity barrier that causes viable technologies to stall before they reach a licensing conversation.
Prosecution Cost
🌐
Marketplace and Partner Engagement
Export any opportunity as a PDF or Word document to share a non-confidential summary directly with industry contacts or at entrepreneurship events. Partners and companies can submit meeting requests directly from your private marketplace without requiring the team to manage each introduction manually. Refreshed UI with inventor filter and full mobile support so opportunities are accessible and actionable anywhere.
Commercialization
📊
Portfolio Visibility Across the Innovation Pipeline
Each disclosure, filing, and agreement now includes a Finance tab with spend and revenue summaries and drill-downs, giving the innovation office a clear picture of what the portfolio is costing and what it is returning at any point in time. Departments and Business Units lets you organize and view the portfolio by research area or program, so it is easy to see where disclosures are concentrating and where the commercialization backlog is actually sitting. A Related tab brings agreements, grants, opportunities, and emails together under a single view per disclosure, replacing the spreadsheet workaround most innovation offices are still relying on.
