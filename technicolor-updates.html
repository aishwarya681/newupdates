Platform Updates · 2025–2026

What's new at Tradespace — relevant for Technicolor

A few platform updates relevant to IP teams at media technology and digital imaging companies managing large, diversified patent portfolios across licensing and active product lines — each maps to maintaining portfolio value and prosecution visibility at scale.

5 updates worth knowing

Portfolio Analytics

Real-time portfolio health, prosecution status, and filing velocity across all technology domains — imaging, video processing, AI, and standards-essential patents. A single dashboard replaces fragmented reporting across legacy docketing systems and gives IP leadership a clear picture of where the portfolio is active, where maintenance decisions are due, and where licensing opportunity exists.

Finance Module

Full spend and licensing revenue visibility in one place. See prosecution cost by matter, outside counsel, and technology area alongside licensing income by agreement. For a company where the IP portfolio is a core revenue driver, this visibility changes how portfolio investment decisions get made — and how they're communicated to the board.

Tradespace Counsel

Flat-fee prosecution through a vetted outside counsel network. For a large portfolio with a mix of active prosecution and strategic maintenance, fixed per-filing costs make ongoing decisions — what to continue, what to let lapse, where to invest — more data-driven and less dependent on billing uncertainty.

AI Filing Event Processing

Prosecution correspondence is uploaded and automatically classified, with deadlines extracted and response summaries drafted. Reduces per-matter administrative overhead on the in-house team so time goes toward licensing strategy and portfolio monetization rather than routine deadline management.

Departments & Business Units

Segment the portfolio by technology domain or product division and track prosecution activity and spend by each unit. Useful for a company where separate technology areas — imaging, video, AI — have distinct IP profiles and separate reporting requirements.
