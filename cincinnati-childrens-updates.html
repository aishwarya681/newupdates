Platform Updates · 2025–2026

What's new at Tradespace — relevant for Cincinnati Children's

A few platform updates relevant to patent teams at medical research institutions — each maps to the operational challenges of managing prosecution across a diverse portfolio that spans diagnostics, therapeutics, and devices, often with multiple outside counsel relationships and unpredictable billing.

5 updates worth knowing

Tradespace Counsel

Flat-fee prosecution through a curated network of vetted biomedical and life sciences patent attorneys. Each matter has a fixed, known cost upfront — no billable-hour variation, no retroactive surprises. For hospital IP teams where prosecution budgets are reviewed annually, predictable per-filing costs make planning and approval significantly more straightforward.

AI Filing Event Processing

Upload office actions or prosecution correspondence and the platform auto-classifies the event, extracts key deadlines, and drafts a response summary. Patent specialists at medical institutions tell us it's where they recover the most time immediately — especially across a portfolio spanning multiple therapeutic areas and device categories with different prosecution timelines.

Finance Module

Spend summaries on every disclosure, filing, and licensing agreement, with full drill-downs into individual records. Track prosecution cost by technology area, department, and outside counsel. Gives the IP team the data to have an informed conversation with administration about prosecution investment versus licensing revenue.

Portfolio Analytics

Real-time visibility into prosecution status, filing velocity, and portfolio health. Filter by technology area — diagnostics, therapeutics, medical devices, software — and see where prosecution is current and where attention is needed before a deadline is missed.

Departments & Business Units

Segment the portfolio by research department or clinical division and track prosecution activity and spend by each unit. Useful when the IP team needs to report separately to different department heads or when tracking which research areas are generating the most commercially viable inventions.
