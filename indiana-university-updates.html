Platform Updates · 2025–2026

What's new at Tradespace — relevant for Indiana University

A few updates since IU's conversations with our team — each maps to what technology commercialization offices at large research universities deal with when disclosure volume is high and the team needs to move fast without losing context.

5 updates worth knowing

AI Filing Event Processing

Office actions and prosecution correspondence are uploaded and automatically classified, with deadlines extracted and response summaries drafted. At a TTO operating across IU's research scale — spanning health sciences, engineering, business, and more — this removes the manual tracking burden so the team can focus on commercialization strategy rather than prosecution administration.

Departments & Business Units

Segment the portfolio by school, department, or research area and track prosecution status, pipeline health, and spend by each. Directors get the macro view; licensing officers work within their own domain. Makes it possible to see at a glance where invention activity is high and prosecution capacity is stretched.

Disclosure Intake via Slack, Gmail & Notion

Researchers submit disclosures from the tools they already use daily. The platform captures, structures, and routes each disclosure automatically — cutting down the back-and-forth between faculty and TTO staff that typically delays intake at high-volume offices.

Finance Module

Full spend and revenue visibility on every disclosure, filing, and commercialization agreement. Track prosecution costs by technology area and research college. Useful for annual reporting to the VP of Research and for making the case for continued TTO investment based on actual data.

Tradespace Counsel

Flat-fee prosecution through a vetted outside counsel network. Fixed per-filing costs make prosecution decisions more straightforward — especially when the team needs to triage a large disclosure backlog and evaluate which technologies warrant continued investment.
