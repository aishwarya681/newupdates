What's new at Tradespace —
built for translational research at UHN's scale

A summary of the capabilities that have evolved since we last connected, focused on what matters most for a translational research and innovation office managing a high-volume, multi-site pipeline across oncology, cardiology, transplantation, and regenerative medicine.

New & Updated Capabilities
🤝
Suggested Partners Agent
Rebuilt AI partner matching that identifies and ranks pharma, medtech, and biotech organizations most likely to license, co-develop, or invest in a given technology. For each opportunity, the agent proposes up to three partner archetypes tailored to the research stage and innovation type, sources candidate organizations, and scores them by partnership likelihood, technical fit, and financial readiness. Adapted for hospital research context, so the results reflect how translational partnerships actually happen, not how they happen in a corporate licensing model.
Partner Matching
⚖️
Managed Filing Service
AI-assisted patent drafting combined with attorney review and filing through a vetted network of 250+ patent attorneys. Provisionals from $1,000 and utility applications under $5,000, drafted and filed in under a week. For a research office managing disclosures across Toronto General, Toronto Western, and Princess Margaret simultaneously, this removes the cost and capacity barrier that causes promising technologies to sit without protection.

Marketplace Updates
Opportunity pages now support direct partner engagement: pharma and medtech companies can submit meeting requests from your private marketplace without back-and-forth email. Export any opportunity as a PDF or Word document to share a non-confidential summary at conferences or directly with potential partners. Refreshed UI with inventor filter and full mobile optimization so the team can review and act on opportunities anywhere.
Commercialization
⚡
AI Filing Event Processing
Upload office actions or correspondence PDFs and Tradespace automatically identifies the event type, summarizes the content, and extracts the received date and response deadlines. For a team managing prosecution across multiple hospital sites and outside counsel relationships, this eliminates the manual tracking layer that causes deadlines to slip during high-volume periods.
Prosecution Operations
📊
Finance Module and Portfolio Visibility
Each disclosure, filing, and agreement now includes a Finance tab with spend and revenue summaries and drill-downs into individual records, giving your office a clear picture of what the portfolio is costing and what it is generating at any point in time. Paired with Departments and Business Units, which lets you slice the portfolio by research division or hospital site, this makes it straightforward to report on what each area of UHN's research enterprise is producing, what it costs to protect, and where the commercial pipeline actually stands. A Related tab brings agreements, grants, opportunities, deals, and emails together under a single view per disclosure, so nothing gets lost across disconnected records.
