Platform Updates · 2025–2026

What's new at Tradespace — relevant for University of Utah

A few platform updates since the University of Utah's demo — each one maps to the operational challenges commercialization directors face when managing a high-volume, cross-disciplinary disclosure pipeline with limited bandwidth.

5 updates worth knowing

AI Filing Event Processing

Upload office actions or correspondence and the platform auto-classifies the event, extracts deadlines, and drafts a response summary. For a commercialization office managing prosecution across life sciences, engineering, and computer science, nothing falls through the cracks — and the team isn't spending analyst hours on routine prosecution tracking.

Departments & Business Units

Segment the portfolio by college, department, or research center and track what's active, pending, and where the bottlenecks are by each unit. Gives the commercialization director the macro view — and lets licensing officers work within their research area without losing sight of the full pipeline.

Finance Module

Full spend visibility on every disclosure, filing, and licensing agreement. Track prosecution cost by technology area and funding source. Useful for annual portfolio reviews, board reporting, and demonstrating institutional ROI on the TTO investment.

Tradespace Counsel

Flat-fee prosecution through a vetted outside counsel network. Fixed per-filing costs make it easier to evaluate whether individual technologies are worth continued prosecution — especially useful for a commercialization office working through a large backlog with limited outside counsel budget.

Disclosure Intake via Slack, Gmail & Notion

Faculty submit disclosures through the tools they already use. The platform captures, structures, and routes each one automatically, reducing the intake friction that typically creates a bottleneck between research activity and formal prosecution review.
