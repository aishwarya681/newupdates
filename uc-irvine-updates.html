Platform Updates · 2025–2026

What's new at Tradespace — relevant for UC Irvine

A few updates since UCI's pilot — each one maps to the challenges a high-volume TTO faces when prosecution is running across a diverse research portfolio and the team is constantly balancing disclosure intake with filing deadlines.

5 updates worth knowing

AI Filing Event Processing

Upload office actions or correspondence PDFs and the platform auto-identifies the event type, summarizes content, and extracts all deadlines. Prosecution analysts no longer need to manually track or log each filing event — the system surfaces what needs attention and drafts the next step. Offices tell us it's the single biggest source of immediate time recovery.

Tradespace Counsel

Flat-fee prosecution through a vetted attorney network. Each matter has a fixed cost upfront, which makes it easier to evaluate the cost-benefit of continuing prosecution on any individual disclosure — especially for technologies where the commercial path is still being determined. UCI can also use Tradespace Counsel alongside existing outside counsel relationships without replacing them.

Disclosure Intake via Slack, Gmail & Notion

Researchers submit disclosures through the tools they already use. The platform captures, structures, and routes each disclosure automatically — reducing the back-and-forth between faculty and the TTO that typically slows intake at high-volume offices.

Finance Module

Full spend visibility on every disclosure, filing, and agreement. Track prosecution cost by technology area, department, and funding source. Useful for annual portfolio reviews and for demonstrating ROI to the institution.

Departments & Business Units

Segment the portfolio by school, department, or research center and filter prosecution activity and spend by each. Gives the TTO director the high-level view and lets licensing officers work within their own domain without losing context across the full office.
