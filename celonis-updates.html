What's new at Tradespace — relevant for Celonis

A few platform updates relevant to IP and engineering leadership teams at fast-scaling enterprise software companies — each maps to the challenge of building a meaningful patent portfolio in parallel with a rapidly evolving product and AI roadmap.

5 updates worth knowing

AI Filing Event Processing

Upload office actions or prosecution correspondence and the platform auto-classifies the event, extracts deadlines, and drafts response summaries. For R&D and IP teams at software companies where prosecution is running across process mining, AI, and workflow automation claims, the administrative burden of prosecution management is significantly reduced.

Disclosure Intake via Slack, Gmail & Notion

Engineers and product leads submit disclosures from the tools they already use every day. The platform captures, structures, and routes each disclosure automatically — so the IP team gets a complete view of invention activity without needing to chase engineers across teams or rely on manual submission forms.

Portfolio Analytics

Real-time portfolio health and prosecution status across technology areas. Track where Celonis's IP coverage is strongest, where it's lagging behind the product roadmap, and where prosecution effort is concentrated — all without building custom reporting.

Finance Module

Prosecution spend visibility on every filing — by matter, outside counsel, and technology area. Gives IP leaders a clear picture of cost per asset as the portfolio scales, so the investment in patent development is defensible to the CFO and board.

Tradespace Counsel

Flat-fee prosecution through a vetted attorney network with software and AI expertise. Predictable per-filing costs replace variable outside counsel billing — important for a company scaling its IP program in parallel with its product development cycle.
