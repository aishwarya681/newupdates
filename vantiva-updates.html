Platform Updates · 2025–2026

What's new at Tradespace — relevant for Vantiva

A few platform updates relevant to IP and licensing teams at technology companies managing large, diversified patent portfolios — each maps to the operational challenge of maintaining portfolio value and prosecution visibility during a period of strategic repositioning.

5 updates worth knowing

Portfolio Analytics

Real-time portfolio health, filing velocity, and prosecution status across all technology domains. For a company like Vantiva managing IP across connected home, broadband, and licensing revenue streams, a single view of where the portfolio is active, lapsing, and commercially viable replaces fragmented reporting across legacy docketing systems.

Finance Module

Full spend and revenue visibility — prosecution costs, licensing income, and agreement-level financial performance in one place. IP and licensing leaders can see the full economic picture of the portfolio and model which assets are generating returns relative to their prosecution investment.

Tradespace Counsel

Flat-fee prosecution through a vetted outside counsel network. For a large portfolio with a mix of active and maintenance-phase assets, fixed per-filing costs make it significantly easier to make ongoing prosecution decisions — including which assets to continue, which to let lapse, and where to focus prosecution effort for maximum licensing leverage.

AI Filing Event Processing

Prosecution correspondence is uploaded and automatically classified, with deadlines extracted and response summaries drafted. Reduces the per-matter administrative burden on the in-house IP team so time can go toward licensing strategy and portfolio monetization rather than routine deadline tracking.

Departments & Business Units

Segment the portfolio by product line or business unit and track prosecution status and spend by each. Useful for a company where separate divisions have distinct IP profiles and the team needs to report portfolio value and cost by business area.
