Platform Updates · 2025–2026

What's new at Tradespace — relevant for CarGurus

A few platform updates relevant to IP teams at marketplace and technology companies — each maps to the challenge of keeping a software and data-driven patent portfolio aligned with an evolving product and AI strategy.

5 updates worth knowing

AI Filing Event Processing

Upload office actions or prosecution notices and the platform auto-classifies the event, extracts all key deadlines, and drafts a response summary. For a marketplace technology company with prosecution running across search, pricing, AI, and consumer product claims, automated event processing removes the routine administration that slows in-house teams down.

Portfolio Analytics

Real-time portfolio health, filing velocity, and prosecution status across all technology areas. See where CarGurus's coverage is concentrated and where it's lagging relative to the product and data roadmap — without manually pulling reports from multiple docketing systems.

Finance Module

Full spend visibility on every filing and outside counsel engagement — with drill-downs into individual matters. IP leaders can see cost per filing, by attorney and by technology area, so portfolio investment decisions are made on actual data rather than approximations.

Tradespace Counsel

Flat-fee prosecution through a vetted attorney network with software, marketplace, and data technology expertise. Predictable per-filing costs replace variable outside counsel billing and make it easier to manage prosecution spend as the portfolio scales with the product roadmap.

Disclosure Intake via Slack, Gmail & Notion

Engineering and product teams submit disclosures from the tools they already work in. The platform captures, structures, and routes each one automatically — giving the IP team a complete view of invention activity across the product organization without chasing engineers manually.
